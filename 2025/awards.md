<h2>Season Awards ({{page.year}})</h2>
<p> At the end of the season, captains will nominate players for each of the awards. Then, everyone gets to vote on who they think should win each award! </p>
<br>
<h4> Most Valuable Player (MVP) </h4>
<p> The player that was overall the most impactful person on the court throughout the season. </p>
<br>
<h4> Defensive Player of the Year (DPOY)</h4>
<p> The player that had the best defensive impact throughout the season. </p>
<br>
<h4> Most Improved Player (MIP)</h4>
<p> The player that improved the most compared to the last basketball tournament they played in.</p>
<br>
<h4> Rookie of the Year (ROTY)</h4>
<p>The best performing player who is 9th grade or younger. (No repeat winners)</p>
<br>
<h4> Sportsmanship Award (SS)</h4>
<p>The player that displays the best attitude, friendliness, and shows spirit of fellowship. This award winner also gets a gift card.</p>

The Annual Basketball Tournament

At the beginning of almost every year, Jesuwon Community Church hosts a basketball tournament for its youth group, college group, and young adults. This tournament has been running for over 17 years, with the upcoming edition in January 2025 being the 15th tournament ever hosted.

Past Results and Stats

This website holds all the results and stats from past tournaments all the way back to 2018. Click on "Tournaments" in the top right navbar and view a specific tournament's details, or select "Players" to search the details for a specific player.
